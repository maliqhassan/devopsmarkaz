Delete your Evenly account

Evenly is published by DevOps Markaz Tech. You can delete your account and its data in two ways.

In the app (fastest)

  1. Open Evenly and go to Profile.
  2. Tap Delete account and confirm.

You need to be settled up in your groups first, so nobody’s balance is left wrong.

By email

If you can’t use the app, email us from the address you sign in with. We delete the account within 30 days and confirm by email.

Request deletion by email

What is deleted

  • Your profile: name, email address, profile photo and phone number
  • Your preferences and notification tokens
  • Your membership of every group

What is kept

Expenses and payments you shared with other people stay in those groups’ history, shown as “Deleted user”, so the other members’ balances stay correct. They no longer contain your name, email or photo.
