Privacy Policy

Evenly, by DevOps Markaz Tech · Effective 15 November 2026

Evenly helps groups of people split shared expenses. This policy explains what information the app collects, why, who it is shared with, and how you can delete it.

Information we collect

WhatWhy
Your name, email address and profile photo from your Google accountTo sign you in and show you to the people in your groups
Details you add: phone number, default currency, region, time zone, language and number/date formatsTo show amounts and dates the way you prefer
Groups, members, expenses, splits, payments, notes and categories you or your group members addThis is the service: working out who owes whom
Receipt photos you attachSo your group can see the receipt for an expense
A push notification token for your deviceTo notify you when someone adds an expense, settles up or invites you
Your plan (Free or Pro) and how many groups and expenses you have createdTo apply the Free plan limits

Receipt scanning reads the text of a receipt on your phone; the photo is not sent anywhere for scanning. CSV files you import are read on your phone and only the expenses you choose are saved.

Evenly records payments you make outside the app. It never moves money and never asks for bank or card details.

Who can see your information

  • People in your groups see your name, photo, and the expenses and payments in groups you share. People you could invite can find you by your exact email address.
  • Service providers that run the app for us, only for that purpose:
    • Google Firebase: sign-in and push notifications
    • Supabase: database, file storage and server functions

We do not sell your information and do not use it for advertising. Evenly does not currently use third-party analytics or ads. If that changes, we will update this policy and the app’s Google Play listing first.

Exchange rates are downloaded by our server from a public rates service; no information about you is sent to it.

Security

All data is sent over encrypted connections (HTTPS). Access is protected by your Google sign-in, and each group’s data is only readable by its members.

Keeping and deleting your information

We keep your information while your account exists. You can delete your account at any time in the app: Profile → Delete account. You can also request deletion here.

When you delete your account, your profile, photo, email, preferences and notification tokens are removed and you leave all groups. Expenses and payments that other members rely on stay in their history, shown as “Deleted user”, so their balances remain correct.

Children

Evenly is not directed at children under 13 and we do not knowingly collect their information.

Changes

If we change this policy we will update the date above and, for significant changes, tell you in the app.
